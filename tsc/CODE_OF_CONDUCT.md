# Code of Conduct

EnerGNN follows the [LF Energy Code of Conduct](https://lf-energy.atlassian.net/wiki/display/HOME/Code+of+Conduct), which is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

All participants in the EnerGNN project — contributors, committers, TSC members, and anyone interacting in project spaces (GitHub, Slack, mailing lists, meetings, events) — are expected to abide by it.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

- **conduct@lfenergy.org** (LF Energy Code of Conduct committee)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and security of the reporter.
